## 🌟 The Complete Cinematic Experience

The animation tells a complete visual story:

1. **Festive Nighttime Ambiance**:
   - Deep midnight blue and royal purple gradient night sky with twinkling stars and drifting sky lanterns (*akash kandils*).
   - Flagstone terrace ground decorated with an intricate golden & vermilion Indian Rangoli motif and handcrafted brass Diyas with realistic flickering flames.
   - Traditional hanging marigold Toran and fairy lights across the ceiling.

2. **Traditional Indian Character Animation**:
   - A realistically animated Indian man in royal festive attire (maroon & golden embroidered Sherwani/Kurta, golden Angavastram stole, cream silk Churidar, and golden Mojaris).
   - Walks into the scene carrying a firework rocket.
   - Places the rocket into a brass launch stand on the ground.
   - Kneels gracefully and lights the fuse using a lit incense sparkler wand (*agarbatti*).
   - Fuse sizzles with bright sparks and realistic smoke wisps.
   - Steps back safely to a comfortable distance, looking up with joyful anticipation.

3. **Rocket Launch & Upward Trajectory**:
   - Explosive launch flare at the rocket nozzle with billowing smoke rings.
   - Accelerates skyward with a brilliant golden particle trail.

4. **Spectacular Multi-Layered Fireworks Explosion**:
   - Detonates at apex with a bright sky flash.
   - Multi-tiered fireworks show:
     - **Golden Willow / Kamuro weeping streamers** with long glittering cascading trails.
     - **Vibrant Chrysanthemum bursts** in royal gold, ruby red, electric purple, warm orange, and midnight blue.
     - **Crackling Strobes (crossettes)** with delayed sparkling pops.
     - Soft illuminated smoke clouds.

5. **Diwali Greeting Reveal**:
   - Center screen reveals an elegant glassmorphic festive card with 3D shimmering gold typography:
     - **HAPPY DIWALI!**
     - **HR LEARNER HUB**
     - **Wishes to All Mentors and Learners**
   - Accompanied by a harmonious festive chime tone.

6. **Continuous Ambient Celebration**:
   - Periodic celebratory fireworks launch in varied colors.
   - Flickering Diyas cast warm, dynamic light on the terrace.
   - Replay button allows viewers to experience the complete cinematic story anytime.

## 🎵 Interactive Features & Accessibility

- **Procedural Web Audio API Sound**:
  - Zero external audio files required! 100% self-contained synthesized sounds for fuse sizzle, rocket launch whoosh, explosion booms, crackles, and greeting chimes.
  - Sound toggle button (`Sound: ON / OFF`) with instant visual feedback.
- **Replay Button**:
  - Re-triggers the complete cinematic sequence without reloading the page.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - Press `Space` or `R` to replay the celebration.
  - Press `M` to toggle sound on/off.
- **Full Responsiveness**:
  - Optimized for mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and 4K displays.
- **Reduced Motion Support**:
  - Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` for accessibility.

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` (or `src/index.html` / `dist/index.html`) directly in any modern web browser:

```bash
# Using Python built-in server (optional)
python -m http.server 8000

# Using Node.js npx serve (optional)
npx serve .
```
